## Scope and automatic repair

The six supported kinds are walls, cannon, mortar, harpoon, culverin and swivel. Houses and watchtowers have no HP and are excluded. Preserve each structure's kind, level and upgrades.

Automatic gradual repair runs only between waves. It pauses as soon as the server accepts the bell/wave-start reservation, including preparation/countdown before `startsAt`, and resumes immediately when the canonical reservation finishes or expires. Do not use the browser's combat-start clock as the pause boundary. During every reserved wave, automatic HP gain and healing-time credit are completely paused across all Carpenter, Workshop and Tech paths and offline time. A successful paid Repair now remains allowed. Between waves, repair starts or resumes from actual HP immediately at the canonical inactive boundary, including when settling offline time. There is no post-hit quiet period or repair-specific last-hit countdown/state. Base rate is `maxHP / 600` HP per eligible second; missing HP determines time to full. Damage subtracts from current HP; it does not additionally reset or roll back prior healing. There is no separate credit bank. For example: at 50%, healing to 60% then taking 10 percentage points of damage returns to 50%; there is no separate credit bank. Offline settlement uses the same canonical reservation/inactive phase as online play.

A destroyed structure remains at 0 HP and non-operational for the rest of a reserved wave unless a successful paid Repair now restores it. Between waves gradual repair can restore it. Any positive HP is sufficient to start the next wave, but the structure is fragile. Wall and tower level upgrades are allowed only between waves and are blocked from accepted bell/wave-start reservation through canonical finish or expiry. Wave completion, reload, upgrade or stale payment cannot cause a free instant heal.

## Paid Repair now

Repair now immediately restores one selected structure to full HP and reactivates it, preserving kind, level and upgrades. It works in either phase and costs the catalogue incremental price of the current base level, independent of damage or payer; wall levels 1–5 cost 10, 20, 40, 80 and 160 coins. Engineer discounts and Tech/upgrade-tree purchases do not alter this price. Engineer's 10% build/upgrade discount remains separate.

From accepted bell/wave-start reservation through canonical finish or expiry, each island has three successful repairs total across all players and structures; the quota resets to three on the next accepted reservation. Outside that window, repairs are unlimited. Invalid, intact, insufficient-funds, unauthorized or exhausted requests consume neither payment nor quota. Wall and tower level upgrades are allowed only outside the reservation window. Wallet, HP, event and quota update atomically. Retries with the same UID return the original receipt and do not heal, charge or consume a slot; after new damage, that retry also returns current state without restoring it. A fresh restore requires a fresh UID. Reject stale phase/level quotes for reconfirmation rather than silently spending a scarce slot.

## Approved between-wave rate modifiers and progression

- Island owner's hired Carpenters are the island-wide source, capped at two, including while the owner is offline. Each adds +100% (×3 maximum); visiting players' personal crew does not count.
- Each Workshop within 10 units adds +30%, capped at +50%. Multiply Carpenter and Workshop factors.
- Wall Tech multiplies this between-wave rate: Quick Mend ×2, Mason Drill ×1.5, Bastion ×3 and retains +50% wall max HP. Quick Mend and Bastion are mutually exclusive; Mason Drill stacks with either. Maximum rate is ×20.25 with Bastion, two Carpenters, capped Workshop and Mason Drill. These modifiers are fully paused from bell acceptance through canonical finish/expiry, including preparation/countdown.
- Remove Captain E manual repair/rebuild, repair-only Shipwright skills, and Engineer's repair-triggered +25% wall HP. Preserve Engineer's 10% construction/upgrade discount; unrelated E build/fire/revive, Captain/Tavern healing and unrelated progression. No special refund, respec or compensation migration; ordinary earned/spent-point accounting remains valid.

## Future visual and documentation work

T59 should reuse the current pixel-style health bars (dark border, green/gold/red fill). Show proportional bars for damaged walls and towers in both phases; hide healthy bars. Destroyed structures show an empty bar, small `!` and existing ruin appearance. During a reservation, bars change only on damage or successful paid repair; between reservations gradual repair fills them. Between-wave damaged-structure cards must clearly offer both choices: a priced instant action (for example, `Repair now — pay 40 coins`) and an estimated free-repair time (for example, `Or wait about 2 min 15 sec for free repair`). Free repair continues automatically while the card is open or ignored; no wait action, repair pause or stored time credit is needed. Calculate ETA from actual missing HP and current approved bonuses, update it when HP or bonuses change, and explain that automatic repair pauses at bell acceptance through canonical finish/expiry so players do not mistake the ETA for a wall-clock promise across a reservation. From bell acceptance, including preparation/countdown, show paused status and the shared remaining paid uses, without a repair ETA. Show exact HP and current fixed price; hide repair offers at full HP. Never round low positive HP down to a misleading 0%. Existing draw functions omit destroyed bars and outside-wave bars, so this is future renderer work. Update player-facing docs after behavior ships and matches the UI.
