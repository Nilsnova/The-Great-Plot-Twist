# How to play The Great Plot Twist

A short guide for players. You build and defend your own island against pirates, trade at the Harbor, and fight one big battle with everyone at Pirate Point.

## The loop in one line

**Earn 🪙 at the Harbor, build on your island, survive the waves, help at Pirate Point, build bigger.**

You start with 🪙 100. Coins are play money only: they cannot be bought or cashed out.

## Getting in

1. On the title screen, press **Play**. The first time, a short voiced intro plays. Subtitles are always on. Skip it with the Skip button, Escape or a tap. **Replay the intro** in **Settings** shows it again.
2. You land on **your island**. A card says: "Build a cannon. The pirates are coming."
3. To join a friend or your class, press **Join** on the title screen and type a code. The field takes island codes (`ISLE-K7Q2M`) and the class code (`HARBOR-K7QXM`). The `ISLE-` or `HARBOR-` part and capitals are optional for a class code; an island code needs `ISLE`. Scanning a QR code does the same thing and fills the code in for you.

If you came with a class code, the game signs you in as a guest with an automatic captain name and no account. Otherwise you play on this device and can sign in from **Settings**.

## Your island

Your island has fixed build spots. Empty spots glow. Walk to one and press **E** (or tap it) to open its card, then press **Build**. The card shows the price and how many coins you have.

| Building | 🪙 | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Wall | 10 | Holds the pirates back from your shore. Four spots along the shore. |
| Cannon | 30 | Fires at pirate ships on its own. Four spots on the corners. |
| Watchtower | 40 | Spots ships earlier, so your cannons reach further. One spot on the hill. |
| House | 50 | Adds 🪙 5 loot for every wave you survive. Two spots inland. |

The cabin stands from the start. Walk to it to open **Invite a friend**.

## The bell and the waves

The **harbor bell** stands on the beach where you land. Press **E** next to it (or tap it) and choose **Ring the bell**.

- A **10-second countdown** starts, then the wave sails in. Each wave is one stronger than your best wave so far.
- **Your cannons fire on their own** at the nearest ship they can see.
- **Man a cannon.** Stand next to a cannon and press **E** to fire at the nearest ship, or tap a ship to pick your target. Your own shots do double damage.
- **Repair walls.** Ships that reach the shore attack a wall, then the island. Stand next to a damaged wall and press **E** (or tap it) to patch it. Repairs cost time, not coins.
- The wave bar shows the wave number, the ships left and your island's health.
- **The weather is real.** It comes from where you rang the bell. A storm brings more ships; fog makes them harder to see.

**Loot.** Survive wave *n* and you get 🪙 10 + 5 × *n*, plus 🪙 5 for every house on the island. Wave 3 with one house pays 🪙 30. A lost wave pays nothing; build stronger and ring again.

## The Harbor

Open **Menu**, then **Sea chart**, and pick **The Harbor**. You see the other captains online here. Walk up to a stall and press **E** (or tap it). **Menu**, then **Harbor**, lists every stall with one sentence each.

- **Market stall:** say whether a real price (Bitcoin, Ether, gold, Apple or Nvidia) will be up or down in 1 hour, with 🪙 5, 10 or 25. Right pays twice your stake; the same price gives it back.
- **Match board:** pick the winner of a real football match with 🪙 5, 10 or 25.
- **Weather board:** today's real weather at your place, and what it does to the pirates.
- **Duel ring:** tap any captain and choose **Friendly duel (3 rounds)**.

A stake needs a signed-in captain or a class-code guest.

## Playing with friends

1. On your island, walk to the cabin and open **Invite a friend**. It shows your island code (for example `ISLE-K7Q2M`) and a QR code.
2. Your friend scans the QR, or presses **Join** and types the code. They land on your island.
3. Ring the bell together. You both fight the same wave: every cannon shot and wall repair shows up on both screens at once. If a wave is already running when your friend arrives, the bell card says **Join the defense**.

Only the owner can build on an island. A visitor can fight, fire cannons and repair walls, and gets their own loot. The visit banner has a button to sail home.

## Pirate Point and the three lamps

Pirate Point is the lighthouse island, shared by everyone. Sail there from the sea chart. If you joined with a class code, you land there straight away.

- Anyone can ring the **Pirate Point bell**. Everyone on the island fights the same wave with the shared cannons and walls.
- The armada brings a **flagship** that cannons cannot sink. Only the lighthouse beam can.
- **Light the three lamps.** Three lamp posts stand by the lighthouse. Stand at one and hold the button, or hold **E**, for 2 seconds. A lamp burns for 20 seconds, and each lamp needs its own captain. When all three burn at once, the beam flares up and burns the flagship.
- Everyone who survives gets 🪙 20 + 5 × the wave number.

**Class goals.** A player who joined with the class code sees three goals instead of the usual ones: **Wave to a captain**, **Help defend Pirate Point** and **Light a lamp**. The Keeper's Lock and the friendly duel are still in the Harbor.

## Your first goals

The quest strip at the top right shows three goals at a time. Tap a goal to see a hint. If you stand still for 20 seconds, a hint appears on its own.

1. Build a cannon.
2. Ring the bell and win a wave.
3. Sail to the Harbor.
4. Place a 🪙 stake.
5. Invite a friend.

## The screen

During play the screen keeps three things: your 🪙 coins (top left), the quest strip and the **Menu** button (top right). **Menu** holds the sea chart, the controls, the Harbor overview and Settings.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch | Standard gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk | WASD or arrow keys | Drag the movement stick, or tap a clear spot | Left stick or D-pad |
| Run | Hold Shift while walking | No separate run button | No separate run button |
| Use (build, ring, fire, repair, light) | E near a prompt | The Use button, or tap the thing | A |
| Aim a manned cannon | Click a ship | Tap a ship | No separate aim |
| Sea chart | M, or Menu then Sea chart | Menu then Sea chart | Y |
| Close a card / pause | Escape | Close button / Menu | B in a menu; Menu closes or pauses |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel over the world | No separate zoom button | No separate zoom button |
| Menus | Tab / Shift+Tab; Enter or Space on buttons | Tap the labeled control | D-pad to focus, A to activate |

Click or tap the game after closing another view if movement has lost focus.

## Keep your progress

A guest account is temporary: the host deletes guest accounts older than 24 hours. To keep your island and coins, open **Settings**, then **Account**, and choose **Keep progress with GitHub**, or, when email sign-in is on, enter your email under **Keep progress with email** and type the 6-digit code you receive. Your captain stays the same.

## Settings and accessibility

Open **Settings** from the Menu or the title screen. Try **Low** quality if the game runs slowly. Adjust master, music and effects volumes separately. Use **Reduced motion: On** for still transitions and calmer waves, or follow your device preference. Enable **Color-blind-safe highlights** if helpful.

Settings are stored in this browser. If storage is blocked, they apply for the current visit only. Buttons have text labels and keyboard focus. This does not promise that the whole WebGL world works with every assistive technology. Name the affected control in an accessibility report so it can be checked.

## Report a problem

Use the project's [GitHub issue form](https://github.com/Nilsnova/The-Great-Plot-Twist/issues/new) (GitHub sign-in may be required), or tell a teammate or your class host if you cannot use GitHub.

Include the device and browser, keyboard, touch or gamepad, what you expected, what happened and short steps to repeat it. A screenshot helps; crop out private information. **Do not post class codes, island codes, tokens, passwords or account details in a public issue.**

## Privacy in short

Quest ticks and preferences use local browser storage. A code you visit is kept only for this tab. Guest play uses Supabase's anonymous sign-in with the class code. Clearing site data or switching browsers can lose local progress; private browsing may not keep it. Use your own browser profile on a shared device.

Connected account features use a temporary sign-in session. The account screen offers **Delete my data** followed by **Confirm deletion** when your account is connected. Deletion removes your own game data, including your island, coins and stakes. Provider security logs and backups follow their own retention rules. Read the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md).
