01 · YOUR FIRST VISIT

Getting started

  1. Play shows a short intro once and the first pages of How to play, then the games card: Create a game, Join a game (a code like ISLE-K7Q2M, or a friend's link) or Continue. Replay the intro in Settings shows it again.
  2. Every game is one island for 1 to 10 players. The lobby shows the code, the link and who is online; the host presses Start. Your island, its buildings and the wave reached are saved, so anyone in the game can continue it later.
  3. With the class code you land in Saltreach Harbor instead: the goal strip lists three things to do (wave to a captain, try a friendly duel, open the Keeper's Lock), then light the three lamps together with two other captains. There the Sea chart sails to the other places of the Saltreach Isles.

Account in Settings offers sign-in with GitHub or an email code. You can explore without signing in; online play, coins and buildings need a signed-in captain. In class, the code from the projector lets you play as a guest.

Your island and the waves

Tap a build spot, or walk up to it and press E, to build walls, cannons and mortars with your own coins. Everyone in the game builds on the same island. Ring the harbor bell to call a wave of pirate ships; every 5th wave brings a boss.

Each wave you win can open more: houses, mortars, the watchtower and crew, then the Harbor after wave 4 (its market stall; the football board after wave 7) and the Tech hub after wave 8, where research gives your towers skills.

Pirates who come ashore go for the heart of the island and for captains nearby. After too many hits you fall: a teammate next to you holds E for 2 seconds to help you up, and pays a few coins. If nobody does, you stand up at the break. If every captain falls, the wave is lost.

Building screens show requirements and play-coin cost before confirmation. An offline or unconfirmed captain cannot make a saved purchase. Wait for the success message and updated building state.

Exchange: understand the risk

The market stall at the Harbor (a building on your island after wave 4) offers Prices and Your positions. A Long position follows rising prices; a Short follows falling prices. Both use 10× leverage. Small moves against you can consume the stake quickly, and a position closes at zero.

Read the price source, freshness and market hours. Closed, stale or unavailable prices pause trading for that company. A connected account is required to trade. Review direction, stake and fees before confirming.

Play money only. Coins have no cash value. Do not deposit real money or pay for a game balance. If a trade is unconfirmed, use Check again or Send again for that same trade and inspect Your positions.

Match board

The match board at the Harbor (after wave 7) shows real football fixtures, source freshness and pool rules. Pick a side and a play-coin stake of 5, 10 or 25 before confirming. A closed or unavailable board explains its status; a displayed score can be delayed. You need a signed-in account to place a stake.

Lantern Duels

Walk up to another captain in the harbor, or tap them, and choose Friendly duel. Each of you picks three sealed signals; they stay sealed until both captains answer, then the reveal shows who won each round.

Weather

Cloud, rain, fog and wind shape the islands. Weather updates at startup and periodically afterwards; a failed update can leave previous conditions in place. Demo or unavailable data can use recorded conditions. The picture alone does not prove the data is live. Attribution appears in the game; wind also affects sailing presentation.

Controls

Keyboard & mouse

WASD / arrows
Walk.
E
Walk up close to a building (about 3 to 4 steps). A prompt shows over it: press E to open it, build, repair, or hold E to help a fallen teammate up. A click from far away only says "Walk closer".
F
Fire your musket at the nearest ship or pirate; hold it to keep firing. Up close it swings your cutlass.
Click
Aim your musket at a spot and fire there.
M / Sea chart
Open the chart, where sailing is open (the class harbor).
Mouse wheel
Zoom over the world.
Escape
Close a view or pause.
Menus
Tab / Shift+Tab to move focus; Enter or Space activates buttons.

Touch

Tap a clear spot on the map to walk, or drag the movement stick. Use Inspect near an object and Menu to pause. In a wave, tap where to fire or use the musket button, and hold the prompt to help a teammate up. There are no separate touch run or zoom buttons.

Standard gamepad

Left stick or D-pad walks; A interacts, Y opens the chart, Menu pauses. Supported menus use D-pad and A; B or Menu closes. Left/right adjusts supported selects and sliders. There are no separate run or zoom buttons.

Keyboard and touch remain available if your browser cannot use the pad. Click or tap the world to restore movement focus. This help page uses ordinary links and controls, not the game's gamepad mapping.

Settings & accessibility

Open Settings from the title, pause menu or in-game settings button. Use Low quality for lighter weather. Set master, music and effects volumes separately.

Reduced motion can follow your device, stay On for still transitions, or be Off. Choose Relaxed, Steady, Quick or Instant text; Show full text skips typing. Try Color-blind-safe highlights.

Preferences save in this browser; if storage fails, they apply for this visit only. Buttons offer alternatives to dragging. The WebGL world is not guaranteed to support every assistive technology; please report a control that blocks you.

Report a problem

First check for not connected, pending or local fake labels. For a new fault, include the page name, browser/device, input method, what you expected, what happened, and steps to repeat it. Say if you used fake mode and whether refreshing helped.

Crop screenshots to the controls. Do not post class codes, tokens, passwords or account details publicly. Report sensitive problems privately to the project team.

Open a GitHub issue

GitHub sign-in may be required. If unavailable, tell your teammate or class host. Following the link opens GitHub; this page does not upload a report for you.

Privacy in short

Settings use local browser storage. Clearing site data, private browsing or changing browsers can lose them; use your own browser profile on shared devices.

The account screen offers Delete my data, then Confirm deletion, when connected. This removes your own game records, including coins and ledger history. While the game cannot confirm your account, contact Nils through the course page for help. Clearing site data is separate. Provider security logs and backups follow their own retention rules. Read the privacy notice.

This help page does not sign you in, call the game API, save your search or submit telemetry. Only following the report link opens GitHub. Local-fake examples contain no real player records or rewards.

The game changes often while the team builds it; this guide follows the current version.
