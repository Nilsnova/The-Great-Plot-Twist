01 · YOUR FIRST VISIT

Getting started & your kit

  1. New game opens The Keeper's Last Log, the solo first act. It replaces your previous local intro progress. Continue resumes that browser's intro instead.
  2. Read, choose an offered action, and check inventory and notes. Show full text finishes the current text without choosing for you. Return to the island closes the story so you can explore and revisit prompts.
  3. Play your own choices. Start a new intro resets your intro. There is no best-answer sequence here.

A connected case service must confirm your ending before awarding your kit. Its arrival screen shows your items, starting place and coins. Kits are True Keeper, Witness, Informant and Stranger; different kits offer different routes into the case.

Account and saves offers GitHub sign-in and save controls, but the ordinary game is still waiting for its online account/case connection. A button or Captain pending is not a confirmed account or kit. You can play the local intro without signing in. Read the confirmed save status before changing devices.

Crews & partners

A crew has four seats, filled by friends or NPC partners. Open Crew and check its connection status. When available, use Start a crew, or enter a code, Check crew code, review Your seat, then Confirm join. Share the code privately.

Muster with NPC partners fills empty seats. In Partners, select an offered order and target, then Send order. Watch travel/work timers and return for the report. Partners can search, pin cards and take light posts. You choose deductions and accusations; partners do not supply hints.

A rehearsal does not create a live multiplayer crew, save progress online or award real player rewards.

The case, without solutions

Investigate the dangerous light at sea using what your crew discovers. This guide does not interpret clues or name useful card pairs.

Searches, satchel & pins

Inspect a search route's price, duration and restrictions before confirming. Paid actions name their coin cost. Wait for the result. Use Pin my card on the case board to share a found card; the board records the contributing seat.

Deductions

Select First card and Second card, then Pair cards. Dragging is optional. Contributions must come from different seats. Read the response and progress indicators rather than guessing what has been accepted.

The Night of Three Lights

When offered, use Call nightfall and coordinate several seats at different posts. Aim with the compass controls or slider, then Lock this beam. Watch the timers together. This uses the case clock; you need not wait for real fog or a real-world hour.

Accusing

When available, review earned evidence and your suspect selection before Present this accusation. If refused, follow the message or retry cooldown. Neither the correct person nor the required evidence is given here.

Sailing & your islet

Open Sea chart, choose an available destination and let the passage finish. Visit your home islet and inspect the cabin, workshop, windmill and greenhouse plots.

Building screens show requirements and play-coin cost before confirmation. An offline or unconfirmed captain cannot make a saved purchase. Wait for the success message and updated building state.

Exchange: understand the risk

The market hall offers Prices and Your positions. A Long position follows rising prices; a Short follows falling prices. Both use 10× leverage. Small moves against you can consume the stake quickly, and a position closes at zero.

Read the price source, freshness and market hours. Closed, stale or unavailable prices pause trading for that company. A connected account is required to trade. Review direction, stake and fees before confirming.

Play money only. Coins have no cash value. Do not deposit real money or pay for a game balance. If a trade is unconfirmed, use Check again or Send again for that same trade and inspect Your positions.

Predictions & sports

The prediction kiosk opens posted questions. Read the closing time, outcome source and play-coin stake before confirming a choice. A weather picture is not a settled result. You need a signed-in account to place a stake.

The sports desk is built but not open yet. A closed desk should explain its status rather than invent fixtures.

Lantern Duels & the festival

The arena gate opens Lantern Duels. Choose the offered rounds, signals and either a friendly duel or a stated play-coin stake. Read Rules for the oil allowance, answer deadline, refunds and winner payout. Signals stay sealed until both captains answer.

When available, open Lantern festival from the game menu. It waits for the first Relighting, then runs for 48 hours. Free lamp reservations and a friendly three-round ring have no stake or coin reward. A reservation does not add a light to the coast.

The optional spoiler-safe hint assistant is not available yet.

Weather & the bulletin

Cloud, rain, fog and wind shape the islands. Weather updates at startup and periodically afterwards; a failed update can leave previous conditions in place. Demo or unavailable data can use recorded conditions. The picture alone does not prove the data is live. Attribution appears in the game; wind also affects sailing presentation.

The harbor bulletin and tonight information have their own availability labels. Recorded, old or unavailable data is not a fresh update. Follow the case's own clock and instructions for its events.

Menus
Tab / Shift+Tab to move focus; Enter or Space activates buttons.

Touch

Drag the movement stick. Tap Interact, Chart or Menu, and tap dialogue choices. There are no separate touch run or zoom buttons.

Standard gamepad

Left stick or D-pad walks; A interacts, Y opens the chart, Menu pauses. Supported menus use D-pad and A; B or Menu closes. Left/right adjusts supported selects and sliders. There are no separate run or zoom buttons. Use keyboard or touch for intro dialogue; gamepad dialogue navigation is not reliably integrated yet.

Keyboard and touch remain available if your browser cannot use the pad. Click or tap the world to restore movement focus. This help page uses ordinary links and controls, not the game's gamepad mapping.

Settings & accessibility

Open Settings from the title, pause menu or in-game settings button. Use Low quality for lighter weather. Set master, music and effects volumes separately.

Reduced motion can follow your device, stay On for still transitions, or be Off. Choose Relaxed, Steady, Quick or Instant text; Show full text skips typing. Try Colour-blind-safe highlights.

Preferences save in this browser; if storage fails, they apply for this visit only. Card selectors and the lights' slider offer alternatives to dragging. The WebGL world is not guaranteed to support every assistive technology; please report a control that blocks you.

Report a problem

First check for not connected, pending or local fake labels. For a new fault, include the page name, browser/device, input method, what you expected, what happened, and steps to repeat it. Say if you used fake mode and whether refreshing helped.

Crop screenshots to controls or an empty board. Do not post clue text, deductions, accusations, solutions, crew codes, tokens, passwords or account details publicly. Report sensitive problems privately to the project team.

Open a GitHub issue

GitHub sign-in may be required. If unavailable, tell your teammate or class host. Following the link opens GitHub; this page does not upload a report for you.

Privacy in short

Intro progress and settings use local browser storage. Clearing site data, private browsing or changing browsers can lose them. A local save is not a cloud backup; use your own browser profile on shared devices.

The account screen offers Delete my data, then Confirm deletion, when connected. This removes your own game records, including coins and ledger history. Other crew members keep their progress; leadership passes to another player if needed. While the game cannot confirm your account, contact Nils through the course page for help. Clearing site data is separate. Provider security logs and backups follow their own retention rules. Read the privacy notice.

This help page does not sign you in, call the game API, save your search or submit telemetry. Only following the report link opens GitHub. Local-fake examples contain no real player records or rewards.
