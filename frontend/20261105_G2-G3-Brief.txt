Contract: createPlace(ctx) returns { id, group, zones, anchors, colliders, lights, update(dt), dispose() }; world.goTo(placeId); events on the existing bus: { type: 'travel', to }, { type: 'arrived', place }, and every hotspot keeps emitting { type: 'interact', id, place }. Sea chart and sailing. A pixel sea chart of the Saltreach Isles: the places below, routes between them, a fog bank over the Teeth, and uncharted silhouettes for places not yet open. Saltreach Harbor (G3). The shared hub: docks, the Harbor Office with its lit window (an important story hint: it must glow at night and be visible from the docks), the Harbor Light, the ferry pier, the jail, the market hall (Island Exchange), the prediction kiosk, the sports pavilion, the signal station, the notice board (the harbor master's reward notice) and the Captain's Table (the crew's clue board). Home islet (L2 visuals only). A small islet with 4 build plots, emitting { type: 'plot_clicked', plot }, and pixel sprites for cabin, workshop, windmill, greenhouse and signal station placed by a plain data list (saving comes later from the backend).
