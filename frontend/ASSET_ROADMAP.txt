Integration boundary
Catalogue entries say whether art is used in Lighthouse Isle, CSS-only, or staged in the library. Only selected island textures preload. Staged art does not activate a feature, make a claim about the story, change the economy or define an API. The catalogue is the source of truth for sizes, anchors, tags and usage; ASSETS.md describes provenance and the workshop displays it for review.

Quality loop
Review source SVGs, full family sheets, thumbnail/game scale, alpha bounds, day/night contrast and the actual island. Revise weak silhouettes and anchors before handing over. Keep the full library separate from the active scene's draw-call/texture cost.

Completed expansion
88 new drawings bring the collection to 108. The catalogue has nine families: 20 original island drawings, 12 buildings, 14 coastal props, 4 sailing assets, 12 nature assets, 15 mystery objects, 8 economy/reward objects, 5 character studies and 18 icons. Twenty-seven drawings preload with the island, 80 are staged, and one paper tile is CSS-only.

Review changes included a tall lean-to workshop, low porch cabin, stronger kiosk glyph, simpler glasshouse divisions, subtle wall weathering and safe roof/flag padding. Two independent final reviews found no blocking visual issue in the new families. The workshop adds 32/64/96 pixel studies; source art remains editable for Nils' next visual iteration.
