GRAPHICS STYLE REVIEW — 5 NOVEMBER 2026

Status: references and recommendation for discussion. No replacement style selected yet.

User feedback
The initial low-poly diorama feels cheap because it attempts too much without a distinct visual identity. Explore a less complex, more unique presentation, potentially more top-down and less 3D. Show visual checkpoints for iteration before building out the selected direction.

Reference 1 — Minit
Source: https://minitgame.com/
Screenshots: https://store.steampowered.com/app/609490/Minit/
Visual lesson: strict black-and-white palette, top-down pixel silhouettes, consistent detail level and clear spacing. For this project, a restricted navy/cream treatment with one gold accent could make the lighthouse and clues stand out. Do not borrow the one-minute gameplay mechanic or copy its assets.

Reference 2 — Carto
Source and screenshots: https://sunheadgames.com/en/press/carto/
Visual lesson: a flat illustrated world, organic shapes and a readable map-like composition. For this project, an illustrated nautical chart could make the island itself the visual identity. Limit decorative density; a flat camera does not remove the need for careful drawing. Its map-rearrangement mechanic is not proposed.

Reference 3 — TOEM
Source: https://www.somethingwemade.se/toem/
Screenshots: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1307580/TOEM_A_Photo_Adventure/
Visual lesson: ink-like outlines, monochrome, simple character silhouettes and compact scenes. For this project, a field-journal look with a gold lamp and clue accents could support the mystery. This still involves spatial scenes; adapt its visual discipline rather than copy its complete presentation or photography mechanic.

Additional reference — Chicory: A Colorful Tale
Source and screenshots: https://finji.co/games/chicory/
Visual lesson: bold, consistent line art and readable top-down drawings. Useful if a drawn cartoon world appeals more than pixel art. Producing coherent original drawings still takes work; the painting mechanic is not proposed.

Recommended direction for a first style study (not yet approved)
A top-down nautical journal: fixed camera; flat navy sea; cream and muted teal land; one consistent outline; original simple building and character silhouettes; gold reserved for light, interaction and clues. Use a few moving wave marks and a sweeping flat light shape instead of detailed water and a physically lit 3D scene. Spend detail on the lighthouse, cottage and a small set of clue objects.

Compare one small playable scene or two original concept frames before replacing the whole island. Existing event ids, weather state and story-location data can remain integration contracts. The current game implementation is unchanged by this review.

All references are inspiration only. Reference screenshots remain on their publishers' or developers' sites; none were downloaded or added as game assets.
